The Network Is Not a Hierarchy — It Is a Response System

Traditional corruption investigations map organizations top-down: find the boss, trace the orders, follow the money. This network does not work that way. It is threat-responsive — each nexus activates when another is under pressure, routing around the attack.

When Kevin Mok burned Banks’s Wayne County police infrastructure, Banks filed false reports in Grosse Pointe Woods — a different jurisdiction, a different police nexus, the same function.

The network has four nexus types, each with its own center of gravity, its own institutional protection, and its own method of weaponization.

Center of gravity: Brian Banks through The Purpose Group Michigan LLC

Banks operates two charter schools that collectively move millions in taxpayer funds through a management company where he is the sole registered agent.

Three percent math proficiency means roughly 11 of 362 children can do grade-level math. That is not a struggling school. That is a revenue extraction operation wearing a school’s skin.

MacDowell ran a $967,964 operating deficit in FY2024-25, covered by drawing down its fund balance. The check register shows $13,970 in gift-card-labeled payments in a single fiscal year, including one $9,945 check.

Nexus 3: Police Weaponization — The Enforcement Arm

Center of gravity: Police Chief Todd Bettison

Todd Bettison is the permanent Detroit Police Chief (appointed Feb 2025 by Duggan). He is also the Secretary of the Detroit Public Safety Academy (DPSA) board — a charter school authorized by Eastern Michigan University. He uses his official city email (bettisont239@detroitmi.gov) for board business.

The police chief sits on a charter school board. This is the structural bridge between police weaponization and education fraud.

Active OIG Investigation — September 2026

As of September 16, 2026, Bettison is under investigation by Detroit’s Office of Inspector General for:

  • Calling activist Jahdante Smith on August 5, 2026, requesting he remove social media photos of Mayor Sheffield with ex-boyfriend Marvin Walker
  • Police subsequently detained Smith and seized his phone under a separate warrant
  • Bettison denied the seizure was connected to his call
  • The FBI is now handling the investigation (per Free Press sources)
  • DPD referred its investigation into both Smith and Walker to an outside agency

The police chief used police authority to protect the mayor’s personal reputation. This is the same pattern Kevin’s case documents — police power deployed for personal or political purposes.

The 2018 FBI Wiretap — Banks on Tape

In 2016, the FBI obtained a warrant to wiretap Gasper Fiore’s phone, targeting 18 people for extortion, wire fraud, bribery, and conspiracy to distribute marijuana. Banks was a “target subject.”

FBI Special Agent Robert Beeckman wrote in the sworn affidavit:

“Fiore is involved in bid-rigging with legislator Brian Banks.”

The wiretap recorded conversations between Banks and Fiore discussing:

  • Banks’s efforts to help Fiore secure an MDOT contract
  • How Banks could advocate for Fiore with a “high-ranking police official”
  • Whether Fiore’s daughter Jennifer would stop pushing legislative amendments if Fiore got the contract

Jennifer Fiore — Gasper’s daughter — was simultaneously dating Detroit Councilman Gabe Leland, who approved $2M+ in city contracts for her connected companies. When Leland asked Police Chief James Craig about the FBI towing investigation, Craig contacted the FBI.

The wiretap documents were accidentally released in 2018, then quickly resealed. They listed Banks, Judge Vonda Evans, Sheriff Benny Napoleon, Councilman Leland, and Rep. Tinsley-Talabi as target subjects.

The FBI Files Are Now in Civil Court

In Area Towing v. Sollars, 2:19-cv-10989 (E.D. Mich.), Area Towing subpoenaed the FBI wiretap files in March 2025. The court ordered the FBI to produce unredacted copies (Sep 3, 2025). Fiore tried to intervene and seal the files — denied by Judge Linda V. Parker (Dec 2025). Fiore was deposed December 29, 2025 and pled the Fifth to nearly every question. The court ordered him to answer by March 6, 2026.

The FBI wiretap files containing Banks’s conversations are now in the civil case record.

The Institutional Localization Principle

Traditional corruption hides behind jurisdictional boundaries. This network cannot — because its members hold positions in institutions with institutional superiors who are now on notice.

When a board member is notified, their university is notified. When a judge is notified, their chief judge is notified. When an AAG is notified, the AG’s office is notified. The bad actors cannot hide because they are institutionally localized — their positions are their prisons.
