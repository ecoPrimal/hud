Filing Philosophy

Kevin Mok is not a lawyer. He is a scientist who filed lawsuits because no lawyer, prosecutor, or agency acted on the evidence.

The first complaint was rough. It was a hypothesis — a first draft. Like a dissertation, it was meant to be refined as data was collected. The first amended complaint was better. The second amended complaint was better still. Each filing incorporates new public-record evidence discovered through FOIA, ICHAT, PACER, LARA, and Wayne County ROD.

The filings will continue to improve because the research continues. The case is the data. The suit is the delivery mechanism.

“I am not interested in settlement. I am not interested in a lawyer who wants to make this smaller. I want jury trial, accountability, and resignations.”

All filings are public records available through the Ingham County Circuit Court Clerk’s office and the Wayne County Circuit Court Clerk’s office.
