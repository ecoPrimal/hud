TCR 22-12 — The Administrator Certificate

October 03, 2026

On August 4, 2023, State Superintendent Michael F. Rice issued Brian Roderick Banks a Michigan School Administrator Certificate — despite Banks having eight felony fraud convictions at the time (a ninth had been reduced to a misdemeanor in 2017).

The certificate came with seven restrictive conditions. This page documents the hearing, the witnesses, the conditions, and what has happened since.

The Hearing — February 9, 2023

The TCR 22-12 hearing was held before an Administrative Law Judge at the Michigan Office of Administrative Hearings and Rules (MOAHR).

The Positions

PartyPosition
MDE DepartmentObjected to reinstatement — argued Banks’s criminal record disqualified him
Attorney GeneralArgued against the certificate — supported the Department’s objection
Administrative Law JudgeRecommended no conditions — effectively a rubber stamp
State Superintendent RiceOverruled the ALJ — issued the certificate with 7 conditions

The Superintendent overruled his own ALJ to add conditions the ALJ didn’t think were necessary. The Department and AG both opposed the certificate entirely. The ALJ recommended giving it to Banks with no restrictions.

The Witnesses Who Made It Possible

Three professionals testified in support of Banks’s character. Their testimony is what persuaded the system to issue the certificate. Here is who they are.

The question: Does Mr. Meihn stand by his testimony that Banks demonstrates “integrity, hard work, honesty, transparency, and compliance”?

An open letter to Mr. Meihn was sent on October 1, 2026.

The Condition #4 Problem

Condition #4 says Banks “shall not be the sole individual responsible for approval of any expenditure of funds belonging to the school.”

But:

  • Banks is the sole member of Purpose Group LLC
  • Purpose Group received $4,285,201 (72.67% of MacDowell’s revenue)
  • As sole member, Banks has sole authority over Purpose Group’s expenditures
  • Holland serves as Secretary/Treasurer of Purpose Foundation — but Holland has a drug trafficking conviction and 5 personal bankruptcies

The question for MDE: Does Condition #4 apply only to the school entity, or to the management company through which Banks channels school funds? If Banks can evade Condition #4 by routing expenditures through a single-member LLC he controls, the condition is meaningless.

This question was included in the MDE formal complaint filed October 1, 2026.

The Compliance Verifications

Under Condition #7, the MacDowell Board must verify Banks’s compliance every six months. These verifications are signed by Board Chair Nicole Wells-Stallworth.

Wells-Stallworth is also:

  • Former CEO of The Children’s Center of Wayne County (through May 2024)
  • The person who received the original MDE investigation letter about Banks’s credentials
  • Board Chair since June 16, 2014 — she has overseen Banks’s entire tenure

The Condition #7 verifications are the documents that attest to MDE that Banks is following the rules. They are signed by a person who has known Banks for over a decade and whose leadership of both MacDowell and The Children’s Center places her at the center of the institutional pipeline documented in this investigation.

These verifications have been requested via FOIA Request #2.
