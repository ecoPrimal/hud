The Hire ✅ Verified

Wells-Stallworth led the board that hired Brian Banks — a man with 9 criminal convictions including 6 felonies — as superintendent of MacDowell Preparatory Academy.

The Free Press (August 2023) asked why:

“Banks’ history of financial misdeeds is well-known to political insiders like Wells Stallworth and her fellow board members. In fact, Wells Stallworth said Banks’ troubled past is one of the reasons MacDowell’s board voted — unanimously — to hire him.”

She told the Free Press:

“It was determined by the board that he is well qualified, as an educator, a scholar, and a community leader.”

Left unanswered by the Free Press:

“whether Wells Stallworth is aware of Banks’ most recent financial problems and what role her family’s relationship with Banks played in the decision to hire him at MacDowell.”

The family’s relationship with Banks was forged at the Black Caucus Foundation of Michigan, where Banks served on the board alongside Keith Stallworth (Managing Director) and Thomas Stallworth III (Director).

Wells-Stallworth retained a national law firm (Foley & Mansfield PLLP — offices in 13 cities) within 14 minutes of receiving the letter. An experienced public affairs executive with deep media and political connections deployed those connections to make a state investigation disappear in a single morning.
