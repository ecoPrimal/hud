The Structuring Pattern

Keith Stallworth (2003): Structured $20,000 through Michigan National Bank to hide drug money. Kenneth Daniels (2014): Structured $19,000 through two banks to buy a drug kingpin a Mercedes.

Different decades. Same crime. Same method. Same network.

Both were former state representatives. Both structured ~$20K for drug operations. Both got minimal sentences. Both remained active in Detroit politics. Both are connected to Brian Banks.
