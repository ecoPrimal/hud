    Root cause of 522

    Cloudflare proxy routes *.primals.eco ? 157.230.3.183 (golgi). Caddy IS running on golgi and serves correctly on port 80. Our network CAN reach golgi on 80 and 443. Cloudflare CANNOT.

    Diagnosis: DigitalOcean cloud firewall or Cloudflare zone config changed during golgi rebuild. Packets from Cloudflare’s edge servers are silently dropped before reaching golgi.

    Fix: either resolve the DO/CF routing issue, or deploy a cloudflared tunnel from golgi to bypass the firewall entirely. SongBird’s cloudflared_tunnel.rs was built for exactly this.
