    2. Spencer Silver’s Weak Adhesive

    Spencer Silver’s microsphere adhesive (1968) had unusual properties:

    Then Art Fry’s hymnal bookmarks kept falling out. He applied Silver’s adhesive. The bookmark stayed. It could be moved. It could be removed. It left no mark.

    11. Why Weak Adhesive

    A strong bond would be:

    • tracking pixels
    • login requirements
    • email capture
    • cookie persistence
    • account creation

    Every strong bond is surveillance dressed as service.

    The sticky note uses weak adhesive:

    • No cookies. No login. No email. No account.
    • The note sticks to the membrane, not to the person.
    • They can pull it or ignore it.
    • If they pull it, the ZK proof is generated client-side.
    • The token is shown once and never stored by us.
    • If they don’t pull it, the note burns or disperses.
    • No residue. No obligation. No binding.

    Spencer Silver tried to make strong glue and failed. The failure was a sticky note.

    We tried to make a login system and failed. The failure is sovereign identity.

    The weakness IS the strength.
