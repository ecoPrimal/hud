yellow light

This is the light we have. People are looking. Almost nobody has written. Yellow is not a home. It scatters.

Green stays. The work, the papers, the chair someone is still sitting in.

Red that is bad travels faster than blue. Scanners arrive first. Blue is the slow listen — a morning reader, a session with a paper. Do not chase the fast red.

The other red is the news we mean to send. We mix that bounce and splash it outside this room, so the clothes on GitHub have a face too.
