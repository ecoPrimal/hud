    Dead Is Food: Ancestor Digestion and the Metabolic Memory of the Membrane

    Twelve dormant repositories are not dead code. They are composted nutrients — ancestors whose science, architecture, and domain expertise feed every living primal. A traveler's guide to finding them.

    Dead is food.

    — ecoPrimal, Wave 193

    The Organism Metabolizes Its Own Past

    The ecoPrimals ecosystem has 12 ancestor repositories sleeping in its soil. They are not abandoned. They are not failures. They are composted nutrients — science, architecture, and domain expertise that the living primals digest every time they build, test, or evolve.

    This paper is a traveler’s guide. You can start here and go back to explore the ancestors. Or you can find this page from any living primal and trace the roots.

    The membrane remembers everything it ever grew.

    The Ancestors

    The Oldest: ionChannel (December 2025)

    300 days dormant. 23 commits. 5 Rust crates. 209 tests.

    Before the membrane existed, there was ionChannel — a Wayland remote desktop implementation for COSMIC. It solved the problem of controlling screens through network membranes when GPU hardware is absent. Five crates:

    • ion-core — shared types and abstractions
    • ion-portal — portal protocol for upstream COSMIC
    • ion-compositor — input injection
    • ion-test-substrate — headless testing without GPU
    • portal-test-client — development tooling

    What it teaches: Tiered graceful degradation. When the environment can’t support the full protocol (no GPU, VM, headless), the system degrades gracefully through tiers. This pattern appears throughout the ecosystem now — every primal that touches hardware implements the same idea.

    Where to find it: ~/Development/ionChannel — the only repo outside the ecosystem directory structure. The DNA before the organism formed.

    The Sleeping Giant: neuralSpring (68 days dormant)

    262 commits. 1,705 tests. 580 Rust files. 50 control modules.

    The entire ML/AI foundation of the ecosystem. When this ancestor metabolized, it was the largest active science crate. Its control modules read like a curriculum in computational intelligence:

    Anderson localization · attention mechanisms · counterdiabatic control · DeepONet · digestion prediction · directed evolution · eco dynamics · game theory · glucose prediction · Hessian eigenanalysis · HMM phylogenetics · immunological Anderson · LSTM weather · LTEE mutation accumulation · meta-population models · pangenome selection · PINNs · spectral commutativity · surrogate models · swarm robotics · transformers · WDM ensemble

    What it teaches: That ML is not a product — it is a substrate. Every experiment validated in neuralSpring becomes a capability that other primals can compose through the BYOB pattern.

    Where to find it: neuralSpring validation summary

    The Personal Science: healthSpring (73 days dormant)

    144 commits. 1,074 tests. 95 experiments.

    A pharmaceutical research platform validated in pure Rust. The experiments range from classical pharmacokinetics to personal medicine to veterinary science:

    Pharmacokinetics: Hill dose-response, one/two-compartment PK, monoclonal antibody PK transfer, population PK, PBPK compartments, Michaelis-Menten PK

    Microbiome: Diversity indices, Anderson gut lattice, C. diff resistance, FMT recurrence, SCFA production, gut-brain serotonin axis

    Biosignal: Pan-Tompkins QRS detection, HRV metrics, PPG SpO2, biosignal fusion, EDA stress detection, arrhythmia classification

    Testosterone replacement therapy: IM PK, pellet PK, age decline modeling, weight trajectory, cardiovascular risk, diabetes interaction, gut axis, HRV-TRT cardiovascular — 9 experiments modeling personal health decisions

    Drug discovery: JAK panel, niclosamide delivery, iPSC skin models, Rho-MRTF fibrosis, affinity landscapes, toxicity landscapes, hormesis

    Veterinary: Canine IL-31 pruritus, lokivetmab duration, cross-species PK, canine gut Anderson, feline hyperthyroid, equine laminitis

    What it teaches: That science is personal. The TRT experiments model a real person’s health. The veterinary experiments model real animals. This is not academic exercise — it is sovereign scientific computing applied to life.

    Where to find it: healthSpring validation summary

    The Hardware Prober: metalForge (109 days dormant)

    5 commits. 17 Rust probe files. Full device registry.

    metalForge already mapped the entire two-house network topology:

    • Flint H1 (edge router, House 1) — ATT IP Passthrough, sovereign DNS, KinderLab guest network
    • Flint H2 (WiFi AP, House 2) — bridge mode, KinderLab parenting layer
    • CRS310 (backbone switch) — 8x 2.5G + 2x 10G SFP+
    • Omada SX3008F (House 2 switch) — 10G AOC trunk from backbone
    • ATT BGW320 — IP Passthrough active, WireGuard port 51821

    Probe categories: WiFi drift detection, WireGuard mesh reachability, DNS resolution chains, DHCP lease audits, SSH connectivity, topology sweeps, port probes.

    What it teaches: The hardware was already inventoried. The tool to auto-validate the mesh was already being built. When we needed a hardware inventory in Wave 193, metalForge had already done the work three months earlier. Dead is food — the nutrient was waiting to be digested.

    Where to find it: The device registry lives in metalForge/config/device_registry.toml — the most complete hardware topology declaration in the ecosystem.

    The PFAS Lab: blueFish (145 days dormant, seed)

    1 commit. Architecture only. But it carries domain expertise from a mentor.

    Professor A. Daniel Jones (MSU Biochemistry, 30+ years analytical MS) personally identified three critical gaps in EPA Method 1633A:

    1. Ion abundance ratio QC — most labs don’t perform it. blueFish does.
    2. LOQ reporting misrepresentation — labs report sub-LOQ as LOQ. Jones personally corrected Grayling, MI water contamination reports.
    3. Open-source tools have unacceptable failure rates — MZmine, MS-DIAL, XCMS all fail. The good proprietary tool (Progenesis QI) is obsolete.

    What it teaches: That human mentors are nutrients too. Jones’s 30-year expertise is encoded in domain/JONES_DOMAIN_EXPERTISE_MAY2026.md. The architecture is the ancestor even when no code was written.

    Where to find it: blueFish product page

    The Metabolism

    The ecosystem has 116 repositories across 6 GitHub organizations. On any given day, ~25 are hot (committed in the last 48 hours). The ancestors are the other ~90 — dormant but not dead.

    Living primals digest ancestor nutrients through three mechanisms:

    1. Composition — a living primal imports types, traits, or patterns from a dormant crate. neuralSpring’s Anderson localization appears in healthSpring’s gut lattice experiments.

    2. Architecture — a seed repo’s product spec becomes the blueprint for a future build wave. helixVision’s MinION pipeline design will become code when wetSpring’s genomics track matures.

    3. Domain expertise — a human mentor’s knowledge, encoded in markdown, waits for the right student. Jones’s PFAS expertise will feed blueFish when the analytical chemistry track is ready.

    The membrane does not forget what it grew. It composts.
