1. The Apparatus

A standard American home in 2026 contains approximately 20 network-connected devices. Each device, without intervention, maintains persistent connections to multiple corporate data centers. The data collected includes: voice recordings, video feeds, thermostat settings, viewing habits, presence detection, device fingerprints, purchase history, and behavioral profiles.

This is not hypothetical. This was measured on a live network with 22 devices across 2 physical buildings.

4. The Response — Negentropy

Negentropy: negative entropy. The organism's immune response to disorder imposed from outside. Instead of accepting surveillance as default, the organism:

  1. Audited all 22 devices on 2 houses
  2. Identified all outbound DNS queries from IoT devices
  3. Traced each domain to corporation, fleet, data center, and GDPR role
  4. Deployed 39 DNS sinkholes at the router (OpenWrt dnsmasq)
  5. Replaced Chromecast with sovereign Gaia Cast (pure HTTP, zero cloud)
  6. Replaced cloud presence detection with local proximity (ARP + webcam + input devices)
  7. Published the full map as a sporeprint — the immune response becomes public knowledge
