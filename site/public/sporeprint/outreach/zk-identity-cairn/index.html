The ZK Solution

A zero-knowledge proof lets you prove a fact without revealing the data.

ZK way: hash(phone + salt) → on-chain attestation. Callers must prove valid reason before the call connects. No proof = no ring.

🔢 SSN

Old way: Recite 9 digits to every landlord, employer, and credit check.

🏠 Property

Old way: Deed recorded in public county records with your name and address.

🏦 Banking

Old way: ACH routing + account numbers sent in cleartext for every direct deposit.

ZK way: Prove "I control this account" to a payer without exposing the numbers. Wire fraud becomes impossible.
