an open invitation from the primals.eco membrane project

You built Apollo for Reddit — one of the most beautiful native iOS apps ever made. You understood something most developers don't: the platform IS the experience. The scroll, the gesture, the haptic — that's not UI chrome. That's the art.

We remember. The whole community remembers. And we're building something that needs exactly what you know how to build.

what we're building

A shared live canvas — like Reddit's r/place, but permanent, evolving, and driven by an AI chipmunk that paints what it learns from human attention. It's at primals.eco/easel.

the easel — 10 layers, one canvas

1. sourdough painting — AI paints stars?nebula?Earth?aurora, ferments each painting as starter for the next
2. campfire — Berry (storytelling AI) narrates visitor behavior, chipmunk paints the stories live
3. null specter — a #808080 gray entity hovering at center, the toggle between black and white
4. hyperlocal awareness — Earth/Moon/Milky Way emerge as humans look at them
5. heatmap — 15 countries as warm zones, path handprints, trust gradient (invisible to chipmunk)
6. personality — ego/id/muse/superego oscillation when the chipmunk knows it's being watched
7. shared palette — humans pick colors, smear pixels (r/place style), colors feed chipmunk's memory
8. immune flush — backscatter from 47K+ visitors as directional light
9. Klein fold — outputs loop back as inputs, p governs scale
10. Klein bridge — SSE impulse propagation across all surfaces (desk, board, easel)

what we need

This canvas runs in a browser. It's beautiful. But it's not native. It doesn't have the gesture vocabulary, the haptic language, the 120Hz precision that Apollo had.

We need a native iOS/Android shell for the shared canvas. Not a WebView wrapper — a real native paint surface that talks to our backend via SSE/WebSocket.

the API is open — right now

GET /api/membrane-pulse.json — full membrane state (47K humans, 15 countries, trust gradient)
GET /data/*.json — 60+ data feeds (campfire, topology, backscatter, thymus, etc.)
GET /easel?monitor — lean-back canvas view
SSE /stream?face=easel — real-time impulse events via Klein bridge
POST /impulse — send a green flash to all connected faces
GET /easel — shared painting state
GET /health — nestgate v0.5.0 on Ryzen 9 9950X3D + RTX 5090

what you'd get

Full access to a live Rust backend (17 crates, compiled in 2 minutes on a 9950X3D). A 10-tower basement cluster with 180 cores, 1.15TB RAM, 196GB VRAM. A membrane that 47,000 humans have already visited from 15 countries. And a chipmunk that will panic-paint for your users.

We don't gatekeep. We don't charge. We say hi.
