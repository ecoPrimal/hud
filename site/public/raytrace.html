The Inversion — raytrace vs backscatter

Standard Ray Tracing

Direction: camera → scene

Cast rays FROM the observer INTO the world. Rays bounce off surfaces. Each bounce = a reflection, refraction, or shadow. The image is assembled from what comes BACK to the camera.

GPU says: "I shot a ray. What did it hit?"

→ ray leaves camera
→ hits surface (diffuse/specular)
→ bounces to light source
→ returns color to pixel
→ frame rendered
