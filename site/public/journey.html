S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: Heart of Chornobyl
Layer 2 — The Zone. Where you learn to survive.

GSC Game World is Ukrainian. They built this during a real war. The Zone is the membrane under stress — anomalies are the threats you can't see, artifacts are the treasures hidden in danger, emissions are the storms you weather.

🔥 Survival tips (the funky stuff):

The compass is your first weapon. Red glow = enemy nearby. Stop. Crouch. Listen.
Unload captured weapons. Equipment screen → select gun → Unload. Keep the ammo, ditch the weight.
Bolts reveal anomalies. Throw them ahead. Listen for the detector beep. Better detector = deeper Zone.
Let factions fight. Watch. Wait. Loot after. A-Life 2.0 means NPCs fight without you.
Emissions: When the sky goes red, RUN to the nearest shelter icon. No exceptions.
Early weapon: Valik Lummox's AKM-74S — top of a ladder north of Water Tower near Zalissya. Watch for suction anomalies.
Stealth: Rain and darkness = less visible. Crouch + walk = silent. Silencer = ghost mode.
