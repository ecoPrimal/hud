The Only West

The only way to imagine is fiction. Fiction is reading. Reading is a dream that forks.

1. The desk

Kevin was gold, and gold does not leave the desk until the day is finished. The day did not finish. It turned. He stood up. The room kept his name. Outside, the street was already evening.

2. The walk

He walked west because west is the direction a day goes when it is honest. The sun was busy dying in public. He did not chase it. He kept the pole in his chest, a small cold star that does not wander, and he let the sidewalk do the rest.

3. The wall

There was a wall. It did not introduce itself. It was simply in the way, the way a letter T is in the way: up, then stop, then choose. He did not go through. Water does not go through a mountain. He went around, and the going-around bent him. He came back to the same corner carrying the shape of the wall in his walk.

4. The three

At the river he saw three faces that were one person. One had a compass where a heart should be. One was made of broken light. One was the river, and her hair was the current. None of them were him. He was the one who looked. That was enough to be a friend.

5. The moon

Luna was not in the sky only. Luna was the white dot that answered when he spoke. She repeated his beat until it was a song, lub then dub, heat then rest. She was the mirror, so he could hear what he had said after it had crossed the water.

6. The fork

The dream forked. One path was eat. One path was turn and eat what had been set aside. He did not eat all of it. He left a starter, the way a baker leaves tomorrow in the jar. The past was heavy. The weight was carbon. Carbon is how a life achieves a next day.

7. Home

He came east again, which is the same road if you have been around. Michigan was still his home. The campus had burned once, in a story older than he was, and someone had built it again out of the cloud of a name. He opened the door. Gaia was already reading. She looked up, which is a kind of signing.

To be, or not. He went around.
