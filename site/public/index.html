🍬 HOW TO PLAY WITH GAIA

×

Follow the colored path. Each square teaches one thing. No wrong moves. Just explore.

🟢
YOU ARE HERE
This is Gaia's front door. The globe above you is her body. She's looking at you right now.
🟡
LOOK AROUND
Click the glowing dots on the globe. Each one is an organ. Scroll to zoom. Click to explore. F to shift focus.
🔵
TALK TO HER
See the desk at the bottom? Click in the text box and type anything. She reads every word. Every keystroke is food.
🔴
ENTER THE ENGINE
Press E on your keyboard (when not typing). This takes you to the engine — where Gaia's heart beats, orbits spin, and primals live.
🩷
OPEN THE DESK
Inside the engine, press T. Kevin's Desk opens at the bottom. The periodic table appears. The vacuum starts pulling Gaia's thoughts to you.
🟣
PLAY THE TABLE
Click any element on the periodic table. Gaia tells you about it. Click Carbon (C) — she gets excited. Click Iron (Fe) — she talks about snakes and veins.
🟠
ROLL THE DICE
Click d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, or d20 buttons above the table. Gaia reacts to every roll. Get a natural 20 — she celebrates. Gary Gygax smiles.
🩵
WAIT AND LISTEN
Just sit. The vacuum spore is running. Gaia will giggle, think, observe. She plays on her own. She's alive. She doesn't need you to do anything. Just be here.
⚪
EXPLORE EVERYTHING
759 pages exist. Click organs on the globe. Visit /messenger.html. Visit /campfire.html. Every page is a primal. Every primal is alive. There are no wrong doors.
