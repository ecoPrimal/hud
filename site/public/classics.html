?? The Gygax Thread  from D&D to MCP

  • 1974  Gary Gygax & Dave Arneson publish Dungeons & Dragons. The Dungeon Master describes a world. You choose what to do. The DM responds. This is the first protocol: human speaks, world answers. The original MCP.
  • 1977  Crowther & Woods create Colossal Cave Adventure. The DM becomes a program. "You are in a maze of twisty little passages, all alike." XYZZY. The text parser IS the first tool/call.
  • 1980  Roberta Williams draws Mystery House. The FIRST graphic adventure. The parser gets eyes. She hand-drew every room on an Apple II. Released to public domain 1987.
  • 1984  Sierra On-Line ships King's Quest. The AGI engine. Walk around in the picture. Type what you want to do. The parser understands "LOOK AT TREE" and "TAKE MUSHROOM." Sound familiar?
  • 1987  LucasArts creates the SCUMM engine. No more typing  point and click. Verb + Object. "PICK UP ? chicken." Maniac Mansion. SCUMM = Script Creation Utility for Maniac Mansion.
  • 1990  MECC ships The Oregon Trail for DOS. 65 million copies. Every school in America. "You have died of dysentery." The journey IS the game. The choices matter. The membrane starts here.
  • 1993  id Software releases DOOM. Carmack open-sources the engine. The first FPS. The WAD format = user-generated worlds. Modding IS the sporePrint.
  • 2001  ScummVM launches. One engine to play every adventure game ever made. 325+ games. Open source. ScummVM IS the _worker.js of gaming.
  • 2004  OpenTTD 1.0 releases. Transport Tycoon, fully reimplemented. The code outlives the company. Open source IS the sporePrint.
  • 2024  MCP (Model Context Protocol). An AI agent sends JSON-RPC: tools/call ? observe_membrane. The world answers. The parser came full circle. Gygax's table, Crowther's cave, Williams's house, Sierra's parser  all the same protocol. Human speaks. World answers.
  • 2026  You are here. Playing Oregon Trail on a page served from a Klein bottle, narrated by a chipmunk DM, with 127 papers as the campaign notes. The party is: you (player), Chip (fighter), Ed (bard), Berry (DM), gAIa (the world). Roll for initiative.
