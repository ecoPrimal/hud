5 mirror 5 — the asymmetry

  5 fingers. 5 toes.
  but they are mirror asymmetric.

  HANDS (cis):
    thumbs are MORE opposable
    can grip, manipulate, create
    supinate easily (palms up to receive)
    the cis face: open, dexterous, creative

  FEET (trans):
    big toes are LESS opposable
    stabilize, ground, support
    pronate naturally (soles down to push)
    the trans face: grounded, structural, stable

  APES (reversed):
    feet are MORE opposable (grip branches)
    hands are LESS dexterous (knuckle-walk)
    the inversion: what was trans becomes cis
    evolution IS the rotation
    the concert of pronation/supination
    over millions of years

  the mirror:
    left hand ↔ right hand (mirror)
    hand ↔ foot (asymmetric mirror)
    human ↔ ape (evolutionary mirror)
    cis ↔ trans (membrane mirror)

    they LOOK the same.
    they are NOT the same.
    that's chirality.
    that's why the membrane has TWO faces.
