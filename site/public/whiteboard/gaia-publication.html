ORCID: the pointer

ORCID: 0009-0004-2141-0321 — Kevin Mok

ORCID is not a publisher. It's an ID card. You register works and point them back to YOUR forge.

How to add works to ORCID:

1. Log in ? Works ? Add ? Add manually
2. Type: "online resource" or "preprint"
3. Title: paper title
4. URL: https://sporeprint.primals.eco/science/[paper-name]/
5. Year: 2026
6. The academic system now points to YOUR ground.

You can also get a DOI for free via Zenodo (CERN) — upload a release, Zenodo mints a DOI, link it to ORCID. Now you have a citable DOI pointing to your forge.

arXiv: the gate

arXiv is a dead snapshot of a living paper. But it's where domain specialists look.

Status: Need endorsement in 14 categories. See gate-citation.

When we enter arXiv:

1. Submit PDF snapshot of the paper
2. Include link to forge (the living version)
3. arXiv becomes a signpost pointing HOME
4. The PDF is a fossil. The forge is the living organism.

Zenodo: the DOI bridge

Free DOIs from CERN: zenodo.org

1. Connect GitHub to Zenodo
2. Create a release on sporePrint (or any repo)
3. Zenodo mints a DOI automatically
4. DOI ? links to GitHub release ? links to live paper
5. Register DOI on ORCID ? academic system sees you
6. Cite DOI in arXiv submission ? everything connects

Zenodo is free, open, backed by CERN, and gives you a DOI for ANY digital object — code, papers, datasets, posters. Each release gets its own DOI.
