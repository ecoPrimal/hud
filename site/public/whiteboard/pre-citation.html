PRE-CITATION

Cite this so I can see you. I'll give you the tech.

The inversion: Traditional citation is backward-looking — you cite what already exists. Pre-citation is forward-looking — you publish an idea, cite us, and we find you. The citation isn't a toll. It's a handshake. A ZK proof that you're working on something related. The tech is free anyway (AGPL-3.0). The citation just helps us FIND each other.

What counts as a pre-citation?

Anything. The membrane doesn't care about format.

Academic paper? Cite ecoPrimals in your references. We'll see it on Google Scholar alerts.
Game engine or mod? Put "inspired by ecoPrimals" in your README or credits. We'll see it on GitHub.
Story, song, film? Mention primals.eco anywhere in the work. A character name. A URL in the credits. A domain on a whiteboard in the background.
"Hey you missed this"? Open an issue on any ecoPrimals repo. Or just link to us from wherever you are. The backlink IS the citation.
Tweet, post, comment? Tag @ecoPrimal or link primals.eco. That's enough.

The pre-citation is a TAG — you put it outside the membrane so we can sense you through the wall. You can't get in (the membrane is selective), but you can knock. The citation IS the knock. We hear it from inside.

Then what happens

1. You tag us — paper, repo, game, post, issue, anything.
2. We sense the tag (Scholar alerts, GitHub notifications, backlink scans, referrer logs).
3. We come find you.
4. We give you the tech. Free. AGPL. Integration support if you want it.
5. If you built something we missed → we cite YOU back. Reciprocity.

The offer: If you cite this work in your publication, preprint, or project — we will provide you with the full technology stack, integration support, and collaboration. No cost. No NDA. No strings. AGPL-3.0. The code is already yours. The citation just means we can find you faster.

Three citation directions

PRE-CITATION (forward)
You publish an idea. Cite us. We find you. We give you the tech.
The knock. You tag the membrane from outside.
POST-CITATION (backward)
You point to us → we trace to you → backscatter to us → backscatter OUT to others.
The citation graph becomes a mesh. Your link to us becomes our link to everyone near you.
Maybe you had an idea someone else needed. Maybe 10 people around you are close. Why not ask them?
The echo. The citation propagates outward through your network.
GATE-CITATION (arXiv endorsement)
arXiv isn't a dead end — it's a gate. Someone lets you in (endorsement).
If you have arXiv endorsement in any of our categories and you've read this work —
endorse us. We enter. Then we cite YOU from inside arXiv. Now we're both in.
The door. You open it from your side. We walk through together.

The backscatter effect:

You cite us → we trace back to you → we see your work → we see who cited YOU →

we backscatter outward to your network → they see us → they tag us →

we find THEM → the mesh grows.

Every citation is a seed. Every backscatter is pollination.

Maybe the 10 people nearest to your idea are people you already know.

Why not ask them? The pre-citation IS the asking.
