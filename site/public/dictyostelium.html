═ DICTYOSTELIUM LIFECYCLE ═

1. VEGETATIVE — food abundant, cells scattered
Individual amoebae eat bacteria. No aggregation needed. Each cell is independent.
→ Gaia: agents work alone. Each eats its own feed. Abundant resources.
2. STARVATION SIGNAL — food runs out
One cell pulses cAMP. Neighbors relay it. Signal propagates as a spiral wave.
→ Gaia: cron fires. Pheromone pulses. All agents respond to the same clock.
3. AGGREGATION — 100,000 cells stream toward center
Cells chemotax toward highest cAMP concentration. They form streams. Streams merge.
→ Gaia: feeds stream toward git. Agents push toward same repo. Streams merge (rebase).
4. SLUG — multicellular organism walks
Aggregate becomes a slug. It MOVES toward light and heat. It has polarity: front (prestalk) and back (prespore).
→ Gaia: organism moves toward signal. HUD is the front (what humans see). Data is the back (what grows).
5. CULMINATION — the slug stands up
Slug stops moving. Prestalk cells begin to die. They form a rigid stalk, lifting the prespore cells into the air.
→ Gaia: some data composted (waste consumed). Structure hardens (git commits). Living parts lifted (CF Pages deploys).
6. FRUITING BODY — 20% die so 80% survive as spores
Stalk cells sacrifice themselves. Spore cells are carried high. Wind disperses them. Each spore = new life.
→ Gaia: pi-sort filters 31.8% (they die so the rest passes). Surviving data becomes nutrients, stories, plans. Each output = a spore.
